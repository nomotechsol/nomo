THE IDEA

Keep scrolling.
NOMO keeps moving.

NOMO is designed to turn fees from its own token into buys of selected newly graduated meme coins. Hold NOMO, and your share of those buys is set at the moment they happen.

25%of the current treasury for each eligible buy
70%of bought tokens reserved for holders
30%of bought tokens retained by the project
01 / FIND

Newly graduated.

NOMO watches Pump.fun, StonkFun, Flap, Four.meme and Pons V2 across Solana, BNB and Robinhood Chain.

02 / FILTER

Skip the noise.

A token must pass the selected filters, including GMGN Total Fees. No confirmed fee data means no buy.

03 / BUY

Treasury to token.

After a qualifying graduation, the system is designed to use 25% of its current treasury, converting PUMP to SOL and bridging for EVM buys when needed.

04 / REWARD

Your share is fixed.

At the buy, a snapshot counts NOMO wallets holding at least 1,000,000 tokens and excludes the liquidity pool. Solana rewards go straight to those wallets. BNB and RH rewards require a linked EVM wallet and a claim.

Why hold?

The appeal is simple: a share of selected buys can accrue while you do something else. The feed shows what graduated, what passed, and what NOMO actually bought.
